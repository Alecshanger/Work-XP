HOW TO MOVE QUEST LOG (APP + YOUR PROGRESS) TO ANOTHER COMPUTER
================================================================

You need exactly TWO files:

  1. QuestLog.html          <- the app itself (this folder)
  2. questlog-backup.json   <- your progress (all realms: Work, Baking, ...)

IMPORTANT: The .html file does NOT contain your progress. Progress is
stored inside the browser you have been using the app in. That is why
you must export the backup file yourself, from that same browser:

ON THIS PC (before you leave):
  1. Open QuestLog.html in the browser you normally use it in
     (the one that shows your real level and both realms).
  2. Click the realm button in the top-right (it says "Work" or your
     current realm name with a little arrow).
  3. Click "Download backup file". Save questlog-backup.json into this
     Work XP folder.
  4. Email / Google Drive BOTH files.

ON THE OTHER PC:
  1. Put QuestLog.html anywhere (Documents is fine) and double-click it.
     It opens in the browser as a fresh, level-1 app.
  2. Click the realm button (top-right) -> "Restore from backup"
     -> pick questlog-backup.json.
  3. Done. Both realms, levels, quests, skills and themes are back.

FROM THEN ON:
  The two computers each keep their own separate save. If you play on
  both and want them in sync, the backup file is the courier: export on
  one machine, restore on the other. Restore REPLACES what is on the
  machine, so always restore the newer file onto the older one.
